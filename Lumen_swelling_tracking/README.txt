# Note 

We have manually tracked over time the nucleation and the growth of cavities (lumen) in pancreatic cancer-derived organoids.

The Jupyter Notebook "lumen_swelling_analysis.ipynb" is used to visualise the dataset in the csv files located
in the "csv" and "blowup_csv" folders.
These folders contain the results of manual tracking of lumens performed on ImageJ.

We perform:

- Preprocessing of the dataset
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Aggregation and analysis
- Visualisation
- Preparation of publication-ready figures

Read the paper on: https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(24)01524-4
The graphs produced are visible in Fig. 2c and Fig. S2a.
